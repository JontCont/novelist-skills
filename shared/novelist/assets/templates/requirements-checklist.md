# Story Specification Quality Checklist

This is reviewer-owned. The agent implementing the novel MUST NOT mark items as
complete on the reviewer's behalf.

- [ ] Genre, audience, target length, tone, viewpoint, and tense are explicit.
- [ ] Premise, protagonist desire, obstacle, and stakes are unambiguous.
- [ ] Ending preference and content boundaries are explicit.
- [ ] No `[NEEDS CLARIFICATION]`, placeholder, or unresolved decision remains.
- [ ] Canon constraints and assumptions are identified.
- [ ] Scope excludes speculative characters, subplots, and world rules.
- [ ] Success means for the planned story are reviewable.
