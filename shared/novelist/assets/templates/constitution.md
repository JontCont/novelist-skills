# Novel Constitution

## I. Specification Is the Source of Truth

Draft prose MUST implement approved story specifications and chapter tasks.
Conflicts MUST be fixed in upstream artifacts before prose is changed.

## II. Canon Is Binding

Confirmed canon MUST outrank outlines, tasks, drafts, and chat assumptions.
Conflicts MUST be reported and MUST NOT be silently reconciled.

## III. Character Continuity

Character personality, knowledge, location, relationships, and lifecycle status
MUST be retrieved before drafting. Returns from death, departure, or absence MUST
be planned, approved, and recorded.

## IV. Traceable Chapters

Every chapter MUST trace to an approved outline entry and chapter task. New
major turns MUST return to planning instead of being introduced speculatively.

## V. Verified Acceptance

Every draft MUST pass structural, retrieval, continuity, and consistency gates.
Only user-accepted chapters may update canonical progress.

## Governance

This constitution supersedes ad-hoc instructions. Amendments require explicit
user approval, a new artifact hash, and reapproval of every dependent artifact.

**Version**: 1.0.0
